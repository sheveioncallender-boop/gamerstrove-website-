A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS.

Do I need experience to visit?

You can visit to browse and ask questions. If you want to join a session, contact the team to find an event suited to your experience.

How do I enter a tournament?

Open Tournaments, select an event and review its details. Choose an available ticket, enter the attendee information and follow the checkout steps for paid entry.

Where do I find rules and entry requirements?

Read the event description before registering. Contact the organiser if you need clarification about deck formats, equipment, age requirements or the event’s cancellation terms.

Can I buy products online?

Browse the shop, choose your options and use the cart to checkout. Current prices, availability, payment methods and delivery options are shown in the shop and checkout.
